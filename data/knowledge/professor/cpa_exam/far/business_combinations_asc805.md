# Business Combinations - ASC 805 Acquisition Method

## Acquisition Method Overview

**Required for**: ALL business combinations (when one entity obtains control of another)

### Four Steps
1. Identify the acquirer
2. Determine the acquisition date
3. Recognize and measure identifiable assets, liabilities, and any NCI
4. Recognize and measure goodwill or bargain purchase gain

## Consideration Transferred

### Components
- Cash paid
- Fair value of assets transferred
- Fair value of liabilities incurred
- Fair value of equity interests issued
- Fair value of contingent consideration

### Measurement Date
**Acquisition date fair value**

### Example
Acquirer pays $500K cash + issues 10,000 shares (FV $50/share)
= $500K + $500K = **$1M consideration**

## Identifiable Assets and Liabilities

### Recognition Principle
Recognize separately from goodwill if:
- Meets definition of asset or liability at acquisition date
- Part of what acquirer and acquiree exchanged

### Measurement
**At fair value** as of acquisition date

### Intangible Assets
Recognize separately from goodwill if:
- **Separable**, OR
- Arise from contractual/legal rights (even if not separable)

### Common Items
- **Property, plant, equipment**: At FV
- **Identifiable intangible assets**: Customer lists, trade names, patents at FV
- **Inventory**: At FV (NRV - profit margin for finished goods)
- **Receivables**: At FV (gross amount less allowance for uncollectible)
- **Liabilities**: At FV (present value of amounts to be paid)

### Contingencies
Recognize at acquisition date if FV can be measured, otherwise apply general recognition criteria

## Goodwill Calculation

### Formula
```
Goodwill = (Consideration Transferred
          + FV of NCI
          + FV of Previously Held Equity Interest)
          - FV of Identifiable Net Assets Acquired
```

### Positive Goodwill
Asset representing future economic benefits from:
- Synergies
- Assembled workforce
- Future projects

### Negative Goodwill
**Bargain purchase gain** - recognize immediately in income

### Example
```
Consideration transferred           $1,000,000
FV of NCI (if <100% acquired)         200,000
                                    ──────────
Total                               1,200,000

FV of identifiable assets           1,500,000
Less: FV of liabilities assumed      (500,000)
                                    ──────────
FV of net assets                    1,000,000
                                    ──────────
Goodwill                            $  200,000
                                    ══════════
```

## Bargain Purchase

### Occurs When
FV of net assets acquired **exceeds** (consideration + NCI)

### Accounting
1. **Reassess** whether all assets and liabilities identified and measured correctly
2. If still negative, recognize **gain in income** on acquisition date

### Example
```
Consideration transferred             $800,000
FV of net assets                     1,000,000
                                    ──────────
Bargain purchase gain (income)       $200,000
                                    ══════════
```

## Contingent Consideration

### Definition
Additional consideration to be paid/received based on future events

### Measurement
**Fair value** at acquisition date

### Accounting
- Include in consideration transferred at FV
- **Subsequent changes**:
  - If classified as **liability** → Remeasure through income
  - If classified as **equity** → No remeasurement

### Example
Acquirer agrees to pay additional $100K if target achieves $500K revenue in Year 1
- Estimate 60% probability
- FV of contingent consideration = $60K
- Include $60K in consideration

## Acquisition-Related Costs

### Definition
Costs to effect the acquisition:
- Finder's fees
- Advisory fees
- Legal fees
- Valuation fees
- Integration costs

### Accounting
**EXPENSE in period incurred**
- Do NOT include in consideration
- Do NOT capitalize

### Exception
Costs to issue debt or equity securities
- Account for per ASC 470/505
- Typically reduce proceeds or capitalize and amortize

### Journal Entry
```
DR Acquisition Expense
   CR Cash
```

## Acquisition Date Journal Entry Example

### Facts
- Acquirer pays $1M for 100% of Target
- Target's book value: $600K
- FV adjustments:
  - +$200K to PPE
  - +$100K identifiable intangible
  - +$50K to liabilities

### FV of Net Assets
```
Book value                            $600,000
Add: PPE adjustment                    200,000
Add: Intangible                        100,000
Less: Liability adjustment             (50,000)
                                     ─────────
FV of net assets                      $850,000
```

### Goodwill
```
Consideration              $1,000,000
Less: FV of net assets       (850,000)
                           ──────────
Goodwill                   $  150,000
```

### Entry
```
DR Assets (at FV)
DR Identifiable Intangible    $100,000
DR Goodwill                    150,000
   CR Liabilities (at FV)
   CR Cash                            $1,000,000
```

## Measurement Period

### Duration
Up to **one year** from acquisition date

### Purpose
Obtain additional information about facts/circumstances that existed at acquisition date

### Adjustments
**Retrospectively adjust** provisional amounts recognized at acquisition date

### After Measurement Period
Changes are NOT adjustments to acquisition accounting
- Recognize in current period income

## Step Acquisition

### Scenario
Acquirer previously held equity interest in acquiree, then obtains control

### Accounting
1. **Remeasure** previously held equity interest to FV at acquisition date
2. Recognize **gain or loss** on remeasurement in income
3. Include FV of previously held interest in consideration transferred for goodwill calculation

## Noncontrolling Interest (NCI)

### Measurement Options

**Option 1: Fair Value Method**
- Measure NCI at full fair value
- NCI **includes** portion of goodwill

**Option 2: Proportionate Share Method**
- Measure NCI at proportionate share of net assets
- NCI **excludes** goodwill

### Election
Made on a **transaction-by-transaction** basis

## Pushdown Accounting

### Scenario
Acquiree's separate financial statements

### Election
Acquiree may elect to push down FV adjustments and goodwill to its separate financial statements

### If Elected
Acquiree reflects acquisition FV basis in its own statements

## CPA Exam Tips

1. **Goodwill formula**: Memorize it
   - Consideration + NCI - FV of net assets = Goodwill
   - If negative, it's bargain purchase gain (income)

2. **FV adjustments**: Always adjust to FV at acquisition date
   - Common: PPE, inventory, intangibles, liabilities

3. **Acquisition costs**: **EXPENSE**, don't capitalize
   - Exception: Debt/equity issuance costs

4. **Contingent consideration**: Include at FV in consideration
   - Later changes: Liability → income; Equity → no remeasurement

5. **Common tested**: Goodwill calculation with FV adjustments, acquisition cost treatment, bargain purchase

## Summary

### Key Points
- Acquisition method required for all business combinations
- **Goodwill** = (Consideration + NCI) - FV of identifiable net assets
- **Negative goodwill** = Bargain purchase gain in income
- Measure identifiable assets/liabilities at **FV** at acquisition date
- Acquisition-related costs **EXPENSED** (not capitalized)
- Contingent consideration included at FV, subsequent changes per classification
- Measurement period adjustments: Retrospective
- Post-measurement period: Current income
