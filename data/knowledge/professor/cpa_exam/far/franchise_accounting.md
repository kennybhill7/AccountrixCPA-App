# Franchise Accounting - Franchisor

## Franchisor Revenue Streams

### 1. Initial Franchise Fee
**Upfront fee for**:
- Franchise rights
- Training
- Site selection
- Opening assistance

### 2. Continuing Franchise Fees
**Ongoing royalties**:
- Typically % of franchisee sales
- For brand use, support, advertising

### 3. Equipment Sales
Sale of equipment, inventory to franchisee (if applicable)

## Initial Franchise Fee Accounting

### Performance Obligations
Identify **distinct performance obligations**:
- Franchise license
- Training
- Site selection
- Opening assistance

### Revenue Recognition
Recognize as **each performance obligation satisfied**

### Typical Pattern

**Pre-Opening Services** (Training, site selection):
- Over time or point in time as services provided

**Franchise License**:
- **Right to use** (if no ongoing support beyond continuing fees) → Point in time when franchise opens
- **Right to access** (if significant ongoing involvement) → Over time

## Continuing Franchise Fees

### Nature
Sales-based royalties for franchise license

### Revenue Recognition
Recognize **as franchisee makes sales** (royalty exception under ASC 606)

### Typical Structure
% of franchisee's gross sales (e.g., 5%)

### Accounting
```
DR Accounts Receivable
   CR Royalty Revenue
```
Recognized as franchisee reports sales

## Refund Provisions

### If Refundable
May need to defer recognition if significant refund risk (variable consideration constraint)

### Substantial Services
If franchisor has substantial remaining services, **defer initial fee** until services substantially performed

## Area Franchise Agreements

### Definition
Franchisee granted rights to open multiple locations in territory

### Accounting
Initial fee allocated to:
1. Services for first franchise
2. Option to open additional franchises (if material)

### Options
Evaluate if option for additional franchises provides **material right** (would require allocation)

## Example: Initial Fee

### Facts
- Franchisor charges $50,000 initial fee
- Includes:
  - License: $30,000 (allocated)
  - Training: $15,000 (allocated)
  - Opening assistance: $5,000 (allocated)
- Franchise opens June 1

### Performance Obligations
1. Training (completed May 1)
2. Opening assistance (completed May 30)
3. License (ready June 1)

### Revenue Recognition

**May 1** (Training completed):
```
DR Accounts Receivable          $15,000
   CR Revenue                           $15,000
```

**May 30** (Opening assistance completed):
```
DR Accounts Receivable           $5,000
   CR Revenue                            $5,000
```

**June 1** (License available, franchise opens):
```
DR Accounts Receivable          $30,000
   CR Revenue                           $30,000
```
(Assuming right to use - point in time)

## Example: Continuing Fees

### Facts
- Franchisee reports $200,000 sales in Month 1
- Royalty rate: 6%

### Accounting
```
Royalty = $200,000 × 6% = $12,000

DR Accounts Receivable          $12,000
   CR Royalty Revenue                   $12,000
```

## Equipment and Inventory Sales

### Treatment
**Separate performance obligations** (distinct from franchise rights)

### Revenue Recognition
When control transfers (typically point in time)

### Pricing
Should reflect standalone selling price (not bundled with franchise fee)

## Repurchase Options

### If Franchisor Option
May indicate lease rather than sale (if repurchase at less than FV)

### If Franchisee Option
Evaluate if affects revenue recognition (typically doesn't defer initial fee)

## Bargain Purchases

### If Required Purchases
If franchisee must purchase from franchisor at **above-market prices**, may indicate initial fee should be adjusted

## CPA Exam Tips

1. **Initial franchise fee**: Allocate to performance obligations, recognize as satisfied

2. **Continuing fees**: Recognize as franchisee makes sales (sales-based royalty)

3. **Franchise license**:
   - Right to use → Point in time (when opens)
   - Right to access → Over time (if ongoing support)

4. **Pre-opening services**: Recognize as performed (training, assistance)

5. **Refundable fees**: May need to defer if significant refund risk

## Revenue Recognition Approach

### Step-by-Step
1. **Identify performance obligations** (license, training, assistance, etc.)
2. **Allocate transaction price** (initial fee) to each obligation
3. **Recognize revenue** as each obligation satisfied
4. **Continuing fees**: Recognize as franchisee sales occur

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Entire Fee Upfront
**Wrong**: Recognizing entire initial fee upfront
**Correct**: Allocate to obligations, recognize as satisfied

### Mistake 2: Continuing Fees Upfront
**Wrong**: Recognizing continuing fees upfront
**Correct**: Recognize as franchisee sales occur

### Mistake 3: License Classification
**Wrong**: Not evaluating if license is right to use vs. right to access
**Correct**: Evaluate nature of license for proper timing

### Mistake 4: Refund Provisions
**Wrong**: Forgetting to consider refund provisions
**Correct**: Apply variable consideration constraint if needed

## Summary

### Key Points

1. **Initial franchise fee**: Allocate to performance obligations, recognize as satisfied

2. **Continuing fees**: Sales-based royalties, recognize as franchisee makes sales

3. **Franchise license**:
   - Right to use → Point in time
   - Right to access → Over time

4. **Pre-opening services**: Recognize as performed

5. **Refundable fees**: Consider variable consideration constraint

### Quick Reference

| Fee Type | Recognition Timing |
|----------|-------------------|
| **Training** | When training completed |
| **Opening assistance** | When assistance provided |
| **Franchise license** (right to use) | When franchise opens |
| **Franchise license** (right to access) | Over time |
| **Continuing royalties** | As franchisee makes sales |
| **Equipment sales** | When control transfers |
