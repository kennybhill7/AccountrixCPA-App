# Consignment Accounting

## Definition
Arrangement where owner (**consignor**) ships goods to agent (**consignee**) who sells goods on behalf of consignor

## Key Principle
- **Consignor retains inventory ownership** and risk
- **Consignee never owns** inventory

## Consignor Accounting

### Shipment to Consignee
**NO entry** (or reclassify inventory)
```
Optional:
DR Inventory on Consignment
   CR Inventory
```
Goods remain consignor's inventory

### Costs Incurred
Freight-out, insurance → Add to consignment inventory cost (or expense)

### Sale by Consignee
**Revenue recognition**: When consignee sells to third party

**Entry**:
```
DR Cash (or Accounts Receivable)
DR Commission Expense
DR Cost of Goods Sold
   CR Sales Revenue
   CR Inventory on Consignment
```

## Consignee Accounting

### Receipt of Goods
**NO entry** (consignee doesn't own goods)
- May track in memo account

### Costs Paid on Behalf of Consignor
```
DR Receivable from Consignor
   CR Cash
```

### Sale to Customer
```
DR Cash (or Accounts Receivable)
   CR Payable to Consignor (selling price less commission)
   CR Commission Revenue (consignee's profit)
```

### Remittance to Consignor
```
DR Payable to Consignor
   CR Cash
```

## Example: Consignor

### Facts
- Consignor ships goods (cost $1,000) to consignee
- Pays freight $100
- Consignee sells for $2,000
- Earns 20% commission ($400)
- Consignee remits $1,600

### Consignor Entries

**Shipment**:
```
DR Inventory on Consignment    $1,000
   CR Inventory                        $1,000
```
(Or no entry)

**Freight**:
```
DR Inventory on Consignment      $100
   CR Cash                              $100
```
(Or DR Freight Expense)

**Sale**:
```
DR Accounts Receivable          $1,600
DR Commission Expense             $400
DR Cost of Goods Sold           $1,100
   CR Sales Revenue                    $2,000
   CR Inventory on Consignment         $1,100
```

**Remittance** (if already recorded AR):
```
DR Cash                         $1,600
   CR Accounts Receivable              $1,600
```

### Consignor Profit
$2,000 - $1,100 COGS - $400 commission = **$500 profit**

## Example: Consignee

### Facts
Same as above

### Consignee Entries

**Receipt**: No entry (or memo)

**Sale**:
```
DR Cash                         $2,000
   CR Payable to Consignor             $1,600
   CR Commission Revenue                 $400
```

**Remit**:
```
DR Payable to Consignor         $1,600
   CR Cash                              $1,600
```

### Consignee Profit
**$400** commission revenue

## Balance Sheet Treatment

### Consignor
- Inventory on consignment **included** in consignor's inventory balance

### Consignee
- **No inventory** reported (doesn't own goods)
- May show payable to consignor after sale

## Revenue Recognition

### Consignor
Recognize when consignee sells to third party (transfer of control to end customer)

### Consignee
Recognize commission revenue when goods sold (agent, not principal)

## Costs: Capitalized vs. Expensed

### Product Costs (may capitalize to inventory)
- Freight-in to consignee
- Insurance

### Period Costs (typically expensed)
- Advertising
- Consignee's selling costs

## ASC 606 Consideration
- **Consignee is agent** (not principal)
- **Consignor**: Recognizes revenue for gross amount, commission as expense
- **Consignee**: Recognizes net commission as revenue

## Common Errors

### Error 1: Consignee Recording Inventory
**Wrong**: Consignee records inventory
**Correct**: Consignee should **NOT** record inventory (doesn't own it)

### Error 2: Revenue at Shipment
**Wrong**: Consignor recognizes revenue when shipped to consignee
**Correct**: Consignor recognizes revenue **when sold to end customer**

### Error 3: Forgetting Commission
**Wrong**: Not recording commission
**Correct**: Consignor records commission **expense**; Consignee records commission **revenue**

## CPA Exam Tips

1. **Consignor retains inventory ownership** until sold by consignee

2. **Consignor**: No revenue at shipment. Revenue when consignee sells to customer.

3. **Consignee**: No inventory on books. Record payable to consignor after sale.

4. **Consignor's COGS** = Cost of goods + Freight-out (if capitalized)

5. **Commission**: Consignor's **expense**, Consignee's **revenue**

6. **Consignment inventory** included in consignor's inventory balance (not consignee's)

## CPA Exam Decision Framework

### Identify the Party
Is question asking about **consignor (owner)** or **consignee (agent)**?

### Consignor Key Points
- Keep inventory on books until sold
- Recognize revenue when consignee sells
- Record commission expense
- COGS = Cost + Freight

### Consignee Key Points
- NO inventory on books
- Record payable to consignor after sale
- Recognize commission revenue

## Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Consignor retains ownership** until sold by consignee to end customer

2. **Consignor**: No revenue at shipment, revenue when consignee sells

3. **Consignee**: No inventory recorded, payable to consignor after sale

4. **Commission**: Expense to consignor, revenue to consignee

5. **Consignment inventory** remains on consignor's balance sheet

6. **Revenue recognition**: When consignee sells to third party

### Quick Reference

| Party | Inventory? | Revenue Recognition | Commission |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|------------|
| **Consignor** | YES (owns goods) | When consignee sells to customer | Expense |
| **Consignee** | NO (doesn't own) | Commission when sold | Revenue |
