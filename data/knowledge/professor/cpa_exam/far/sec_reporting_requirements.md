# SEC Reporting Requirements

## Overview

**Established**: 1934 by Securities Exchange Act

**Mission**:
- Protect investors
- Maintain fair, orderly, and efficient markets
- Facilitate capital formation

**Authority**: Enforce federal securities laws and regulate securities industry

**EDGAR System**: Electronic Data Gathering, Analysis, and Retrieval - free public access at sec.gov/edgar

## Who Must File with SEC

**Public companies must file if**:
- Listed on stock exchanges (NYSE, NASDAQ, etc.)
- Assets ≥ $10M AND shareholders ≥ 500 (or ≥ 2,000 total shareholders)
- Conducting public offerings

## Major SEC Forms

### Registration Statements

| Form | Purpose | When Used | Content |
|------|---------|-----------|---------|
| **S-1** | General registration form (IPO) | Initial public offering or major offering | Detailed company info, business, financials, risk factors |
| **S-3** | Short form for established issuers (shelf registration) | Company meets eligibility (well-known issuer, reporting 12+ months) | Incorporates by reference, faster process |
| **S-4** | Business combinations and exchange offers | Mergers, acquisitions where securities issued | Transaction details, combined entity info |
| **S-8** | Employee benefit plans | Register shares for stock options, ESPP | Plan details, shares available |

### Periodic Reports

#### Form 10-K (Annual Report)

**Description**: Comprehensive annual report with audited financial statements

**Due**:
- **Large Accelerated Filer** (public float ≥ $700M): **60 days**
- **Accelerated Filer** (float ≥ $75M but < $700M): **75 days**
- **Non-Accelerated Filer** (float < $75M): **90 days**

**Content**:
- **Audited financial statements** (3 years income statement, 2 years balance sheet)
- Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)
- Risk factors
- Business description
- Corporate governance
- Executive compensation
- Internal control report (SOX 404)
- Exhibits and schedules

**Signatures**: CEO, CFO, principal accounting officer

#### Form 10-Q (Quarterly Report)

**Description**: Quarterly report with unaudited interim financial statements

**Due**:
- **Large Accelerated and Accelerated Filers**: **40 days**
- **Non-Accelerated Filers**: **45 days**

**Content**:
- **Unaudited interim financials** (comparative)
- MD&A for quarter
- Updates on material changes
- Disclosure controls certifications

**Quarters**: First 3 quarters only (4th quarter covered by 10-K)

#### Form 8-K (Current Report)

**Description**: Report of material events

**Due**: Generally **4 business days** after event

**Material Events** (examples):
- Entry into or termination of material agreement
- Bankruptcy or receivership
- Completion of acquisition or disposition
- **Results of operations** and financial condition (earnings release)
- Creation of direct financial obligation
- Triggering events that accelerate obligations
- Exit or disposal costs
- Changes in accountants or disagreements on accounting
- Amendments to articles or bylaws
- Changes in fiscal year
- **Officer/director changes**

**Key Point**: 8-K provides timely disclosure of important events between periodic reports

### Proxy Statements

**Form DEF 14A** (Definitive Proxy Statement):
- **When**: Annual shareholder meeting
- **Content**:
  - Board director elections
  - Executive compensation disclosures
  - Proposals for shareholder vote
  - Auditor ratification
  - Say-on-pay votes

### Beneficial Ownership Reports

| Form | Purpose | When | Due Date |
|------|---------|------|----------|
| **Schedule 13D** | Beneficial ownership > 5% | Acquirer obtains > 5% with intent to influence control | 10 days after crossing 5% |
| **Schedule 13G** | Beneficial ownership > 5% (passive) | Passive investor (no control intent) | 45 days after year end |
| **Form 4** | Insider transaction report | Officers, directors, 10%+ owners trade | 2 business days after transaction |

## Key SEC Regulations

### Regulation S-X

**Purpose**: Form and content of **financial statements** filed with SEC

**Scope**:
- Specifies accounting standards to follow
- Financial statement presentation requirements
- Footnote disclosures required
- Specific rules by industry

**Key Articles**:
- **Article 5**: Commercial and industrial companies
- **Article 9**: Bank holding companies
- **Article 10**: Interim financial statements

### Regulation S-K

**Purpose**: **Non-financial statement** disclosure requirements

**Scope**:
- Business description
- Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)
- Risk factors
- Executive compensation
- Corporate governance disclosures
- Legal proceedings
- Market for common equity

**Item 303**: MD&A requirements (heavily tested concept)

### Regulation FD (Fair Disclosure)

**Purpose**: Prohibit selective disclosure of material nonpublic information

**Requirement**:
- If material information disclosed to analysts/investors:
  - **Intentional** disclosure → Must **simultaneously** disclose publicly
  - **Unintentional** disclosure → Must **promptly** disclose publicly (within 24 hours)

**Rationale**: Level playing field for all investors

## Accelerated Filer Status

**Affects**: Filing deadlines for 10-K and 10-Q

**Determined by**: Public float (market value of publicly held shares)

| Category | Criteria | 10-K Deadline | 10-Q Deadline |
|----------|----------|---------------|---------------|
| **Large Accelerated Filer** | Public float ≥ $700M | 60 days | 40 days |
| **Accelerated Filer** | Float ≥ $75M but < $700M | 75 days | 40 days |
| **Non-Accelerated Filer** | Float < $75M | 90 days | 45 days |
| **Smaller Reporting Company** | Float < $250M or revenues < $100M | Varies | Reduced disclosures allowed |

**Public Float**: Calculated as of last business day of most recently completed second fiscal quarter

## Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX) - Key Provisions

### Section 302 - Certifications

**Requirement**: CEO and CFO must certify:
- They have reviewed the report
- Report does not contain material misstatements or omissions
- Financial statements fairly present financial condition and results
- They are responsible for establishing and maintaining disclosure controls
- They have disclosed all significant deficiencies in internal controls to auditors and audit committee

**Consequence**: Personal accountability for executives

### Section 404 - Internal Control Assessment

**Management's Responsibility**:
- Assess effectiveness of internal control over financial reporting (ICFR)
- Report on ICFR in annual report

**Auditor's Responsibility**:
- **Large accelerated filers**: Auditor must **attest** to management's assessment
- **Smaller companies**: Auditor attestation not required

**Key Point**: This is one of most costly SOX provisions

### Section 906 - Criminal Penalties

**Requirement**: CEO and CFO criminal penalties for knowingly certifying false statements

**Penalties**:
- Up to $5M fine and 20 years imprisonment for willful violations

### PCAOB (Public Company Accounting Oversight Board)

**Created by**: Sarbanes-Oxley Act

**Purpose**: Oversee auditors of public companies

**Responsibilities**:
- Register public accounting firms
- Establish auditing standards
- Inspect registered firms
- Enforce compliance

## Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)

**Required in**: 10-K, 10-Q, proxy statements

**Purpose**: Management's perspective on financial condition and results

**Content**:

**1. Liquidity and Capital Resources**:
- Sources and uses of cash
- Debt obligations and covenants
- Off-balance-sheet arrangements
- Contractual obligations table

**2. Results of Operations**:
- Discussion of revenues, costs, and expenses
- Comparison to prior periods
- Segment analysis if applicable

**3. Known Trends and Uncertainties**:
- Forward-looking information
- Material trends affecting business
- Impact of inflation, seasonality

**4. Critical Accounting Estimates**:
- Key judgments and estimates
- Impact on financial statements

**5. Recent Accounting Pronouncements**:
- New standards adopted or to be adopted

**Key Point**: MD&A should provide forward-looking analysis, not just historical recap

## CPA Exam Strategies

### Forms to Remember

**Periodic Reports**:
- **10-K**: Annual, **audited**, comprehensive
- **10-Q**: Quarterly, **unaudited**, 3 quarters only
- **8-K**: **Current events**, 4 days

**Registration**:
- **S-1**: IPO registration (general form)
- **S-3**: Shelf registration (established issuers)
- **S-8**: Employee stock plans

**Proxy**:
- **DEF 14A**: Proxy statement for annual meeting

### Key Regulations

- **Regulation S-X**: **Financial statement** form and content
- **Regulation S-K**: **Non-financial** disclosures
- **Regulation FD**: **Fair disclosure** (no selective disclosure)

### Testing Approach

**SEC topics are LOW weight** on FAR exam (1-3 questions)

**When tested**:
- Usually **conceptual** questions (which form for what purpose?)
- Not detailed application
- Focus on basic forms and filing requirements

**Quick Memorization**:
- **10-K = Annual = Audited**
- **10-Q = Quarterly = Unaudited**
- **8-K = Events = 4 days**
- **S-X = Financials**, **S-K = Non-financials**

### Common Questions

**Q**: What form for IPO?
**A**: S-1

**Q**: What form reports quarterly results?
**A**: 10-Q

**Q**: What form reports material events like officer changes?
**A**: 8-K

**Q**: What regulation governs financial statement presentation?
**A**: Regulation S-X

**Q**: What does MD&A stand for?
**A**: Management's Discussion and Analysis

## Summary

### Key Points

- **SEC** regulates public company reporting under securities laws
- **Periodic reports**: 10-K (annual, audited), 10-Q (quarterly, unaudited), 8-K (current events)
- **Registration**: S-1 for IPO, S-3 for shelf registration
- **Proxy**: DEF 14A for annual shareholder meetings
- **Regulations**: S-X (financial statements), S-K (non-financial disclosures), FD (fair disclosure)
- **EDGAR**: Electronic filing system with free public access
- **Filing deadlines** vary by accelerated filer status (based on public float)
- **SOX provisions**: Section 302 (CEO/CFO certifications), Section 404 (internal control assessment)
- **PCAOB**: Oversees auditors of public companies
- **MD&A**: Management's forward-looking discussion of financial condition and results

### Quick Reference Chart

| Form | Type | Content | Frequency |
|------|------|---------|-----------|
| 10-K | Periodic | Annual report, audited | Annual (60-90 days) |
| 10-Q | Periodic | Quarterly, unaudited | 3 quarters (40-45 days) |
| 8-K | Current | Material events | As events occur (4 days) |
| S-1 | Registration | IPO registration | One-time |
| DEF 14A | Proxy | Annual meeting proxy | Annual |

### Exam Focus

Since SEC topics are **low-weight**, focus on:
- Basic form identification (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, S-1)
- Reg S-X vs. S-K distinction
- Simple fact patterns about what form to file
