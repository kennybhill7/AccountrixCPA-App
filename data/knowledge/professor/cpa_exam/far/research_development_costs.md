# Research and Development Costs - ASC 730

## General Rule
**EXPENSE ALL research and development costs as incurred**

## Definitions

### Research
Planned investigation undertaken to discover new knowledge

### Development
Translation of research findings into plan or design for new product, process, or system

## R&D Activities (Expense)
- Laboratory research
- Design and testing of prototypes
- Design of tools, jigs, molds involving new technology
- Design of pilot plant not useful for commercial production
- Engineering activity to advance product to production stage

## NOT R&D
- Routine product improvements
- Marketing research
- Quality control testing
- Troubleshooting during commercial production
- Legal work on patents (capitalize as intangible)
- Engineering follow-through in early production phase

## Exceptions to Expensing

### 1. Materials/Equipment with Alternative Future Use

**Rule**: If materials/equipment acquired for R&D have **ALTERNATIVE FUTURE USE**, capitalize as asset and depreciate

**Example**: Purchase equipment $100K for R&D project. Equipment can be used for other projects after.
- Capitalize $100K as PPE
- Depreciate over useful life
- Allocate depreciation to R&D expense

**If NO Alternative Use**: Expense immediately as R&D

### 2. R&D for Others Under Contract

**Rule**: If performing R&D for others under contract, capitalize costs as inventory or contract asset (NOT R&D expense)

**Reason**: Revenue-generating activity, not own R&D

### 3. Acquired in Business Combination

**Rule**: R&D assets acquired in business combination recognized at fair value (in-process R&D)

**Subsequent**:
- If NO alternative use after acquisition → Immediately expense
- If alternative use → Capitalize and evaluate useful life

## Software Development Costs

### Internal-Use Software
- Expense until application development stage
- Capitalize development stage costs
- Expense post-implementation

### Software for Sale
- Expense until technological feasibility
- Capitalize thereafter until product available
- (ASC 985 - separate guidance)

## Components of R&D Costs

### Included in R&D Expense
- Salaries of R&D personnel
- Materials consumed in R&D
- Depreciation of equipment used in R&D (if has alternative use)
- Allocated overhead (utilities, rent) for R&D facilities
- Costs of services performed by others for R&D

### Excluded from R&D Expense
- General and administrative costs not related to R&D
- Marketing costs
- Post-production costs (quality control, troubleshooting)

## Disclosure Requirements
Disclose total R&D expense charged to expense for the period

## Calculation Example

### Facts
Company incurs:
- $200K R&D salaries
- $50K materials (no alternative use)
- $100K equipment (has alternative future uses, 5-year life)
- $30K allocated overhead
- $20K patent legal fees

### Classification

**R&D Expense**:
```
Salaries:                    $200,000
Materials:                     50,000
Equipment depreciation:        20,000  ($100K / 5)
Overhead:                      30,000
────────────────────────────────────
Total R&D Expense:           $300,000
```

**Intangible Asset**: $20,000 patent legal fees (NOT R&D expense)

**PPE**: $100,000 equipment (capitalize, depreciate)

### Entries
```
DR R&D Expense                     $300,000
   CR Salaries Payable                        $200,000
   CR Materials                                 50,000
   CR Accumulated Depreciation                  20,000
   CR Overhead Allocated                        30,000

DR Equipment                       $100,000
   CR Cash                                     $100,000

DR Patent                           $20,000
   CR Cash                                      $20,000
```

## Comparison Table

| Item | Treatment | Examples |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **R&D Costs** | Expense as incurred | Lab research, Prototype design, Pilot plant |
| **Startup Costs** | Expense as incurred | Pre-opening costs, Training, Organization costs |
| **Patent Legal Costs** | Capitalize as intangible | Legal fees to obtain patent, Registration fees |
| **Equipment with Alternative Use** | Capitalize, depreciate | R&D equipment usable for other purposes |

## CPA Exam Tips

1. **Default**: EXPENSE all R&D costs as incurred

2. **Exception**: Materials/equipment with **ALTERNATIVE FUTURE USE** → Capitalize, depreciate

3. **Patent legal fees** NOT R&D (capitalize as intangible asset)

4. **R&D for others** under contract → Capitalize (not R&D expense)

5. **Software development**: Expense until technological feasibility (ASC 985)

6. **Marketing research** NOT R&D (expense as marketing cost)

7. **Routine product improvements** NOT R&D

## Decision Framework

1. Is it R&D activity? (lab research, prototype, pilot plant, design)

2. If YES: Expense as incurred (default)

3. Exception check:
   - Alternative future use? → Capitalize, depreciate
   - R&D for others? → Capitalize as contract asset
   - Patent legal fees? → Capitalize as intangible

4. If NOT R&D: Check if marketing, quality control, troubleshooting (expense as other)

## Common Exam Scenarios

1. **Equipment purchase**: Check for alternative use
   - Capitalize vs. expense

2. **Patent costs**: Legal fees capitalize, R&D to develop invention expense

3. **Software**: Check technological feasibility milestone
   - Expense before, capitalize after

4. **Services**: R&D services purchased from third party → R&D expense

## Common Mistakes

1. Capitalizing R&D costs (should expense unless exception applies)

2. Treating patent legal fees as R&D expense (should capitalize as intangible)

3. Expensing equipment with alternative use (should capitalize)

4. Including marketing research in R&D (separate category)

## Summary

### Key Points
- **General rule**: Expense ALL R&D costs as incurred
- **Exception**: Materials/equipment with alternative future use → Capitalize, depreciate
- **Exception**: R&D for others under contract → Capitalize as contract asset
- **Patent legal fees** → Capitalize as intangible (NOT R&D expense)
- **Software**: Expense until technological feasibility, capitalize thereafter
- **Not R&D**: Marketing research, quality control, routine improvements
- **Disclose** total R&D expense for period
