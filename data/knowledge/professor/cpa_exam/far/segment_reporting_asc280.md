# Segment Reporting - ASC 280

## Overview

**Segment reporting**: Disclosure of financial information about **operating segments** of a business

**Purpose**: Provides users with insight into different parts of the business that may have different risks, returns, and growth prospects

## Who Must Report Segments?

**Public companies only**:
- Companies with publicly traded debt or equity securities
- Required to file with SEC

**Private companies**: Not required to report segment information

## Management Approach

**Key concept**: Segments based on how **management internally organizes and evaluates** the business

**CODM**: Chief Operating Decision Maker
- Person or group that assesses performance and allocates resources
- Segment structure follows CODM's view of the business

## Operating Segment Definition

### Three Required Characteristics

An operating segment must meet **ALL THREE**:

1. **Engages in business activities** from which it earns revenues and incurs expenses

2. **Operating results regularly reviewed by CODM** for:
   - Performance assessment
   - Resource allocation decisions

3. **Discrete financial information is available**

### Examples of Operating Segments
- Geographic divisions (North America, Europe, Asia)
- Product lines (Consumer products, Industrial products)
- Customer types (Retail, Wholesale, Government)
- Operating divisions (Manufacturing, Distribution, Services)

## Quantitative Thresholds (10% Tests)

### Three Tests

An operating segment is **reportable** if it meets **ANY ONE** of the following:

### 1. Revenue Test
```
Segment Revenue (including intersegment) ≥ 10% of Combined Revenue
```

**Note**: Includes **both external and intersegment** revenue

### 2. Profit or Loss Test
```
Absolute Value of Segment P/L ≥ 10% of Greater of:
  (a) Combined profit of all profitable segments, OR
  (b) Combined loss of all loss segments
```

**Key**: Use **absolute value** and compare to the **greater** of (a) or (b)

### 3. Assets Test
```
Segment Assets ≥ 10% of Combined Assets of All Segments
```

### Summary
**Reportable if meets ANY ONE of the three 10% tests**

## The 75% Test

### Requirement
**Reportable segments' external revenue** must be ≥ 75% of total **consolidated revenue**

### Purpose
Ensures sufficient coverage of the entity's operations

### If Not Met
Identify additional segments as reportable until the 75% threshold is achieved

### Formula
```
Sum of Reportable Segments' External Revenue
─────────────────────────────────────────────── ≥ 75%
Total Consolidated Revenue
```

## Aggregation Criteria

### When Allowed
May aggregate **similar** operating segments into a single reportable segment

### Requirements
Segments must have:
- **Similar economic characteristics**, AND
- Similar in **all** of the following:
  1. Nature of products/services
  2. Nature of production processes
  3. Type or class of customer
  4. Methods of distribution
  5. If applicable, nature of regulatory environment

## Calculation Example

### Facts
Company has 5 operating segments:

| Segment | Total Revenue | External | Intersegment | Profit/(Loss) | Assets |
|---------|--------------|----------|--------------|---------------|---------|
| A | $100M | $90M | $10M | $20M | $200M |
| B | $80M | $80M | $0 | $15M | $150M |
| C | $50M | $40M | $10M | $(5M) | $80M |
| D | $30M | $30M | $0 | $8M | $60M |
| E | $20M | $15M | $5M | $(3M) | $40M |

### Step 1: Calculate Combined Totals

**Combined Revenue** (including intersegment):
```
$100M + $80M + $50M + $30M + $20M = $280M
```

**Combined Profit of Profitable Segments**:
```
$20M + $15M + $8M = $43M
```

**Combined Loss of Loss Segments**:
```
$5M + $3M = $8M (absolute values)
```

**Combined Assets**:
```
$200M + $150M + $80M + $60M + $40M = $530M
```

**External Revenue**:
```
$90M + $80M + $40M + $30M + $15M = $255M
```

### Step 2: Calculate 10% Thresholds

**Revenue threshold**:
```
10% × $280M = $28M
```

**Profit/Loss threshold**:
```
10% × $43M = $4.3M (using greater of $43M vs. $8M)
```

**Assets threshold**:
```
10% × $530M = $53M
```

### Step 3: Test Each Segment

**Segment A**:
- Revenue: $100M > $28M ✓
- Profit: $20M > $4.3M ✓
- Assets: $200M > $53M ✓
- **REPORTABLE** (meets all three tests)

**Segment B**:
- Revenue: $80M > $28M ✓
- Profit: $15M > $4.3M ✓
- Assets: $150M > $53M ✓
- **REPORTABLE**

**Segment C**:
- Revenue: $50M > $28M ✓
- Loss: $5M > $4.3M ✓
- Assets: $80M > $53M ✓
- **REPORTABLE**

**Segment D**:
- Revenue: $30M > $28M ✓
- Profit: $8M > $4.3M ✓
- Assets: $60M > $53M ✓
- **REPORTABLE**

**Segment E**:
- Revenue: $20M < $28M ✗
- Loss: $3M < $4.3M ✗
- Assets: $40M < $53M ✗
- **NOT reportable** (meets none of the tests)

### Step 4: Apply 75% Test

**External revenue of reportable segments** (A + B + C + D):
```
$90M + $80M + $40M + $30M = $240M
```

**Percentage**:
```
$240M / $255M = 94.1% > 75% ✓
```

**Conclusion**: The four reportable segments (A, B, C, D) are sufficient. No need to add Segment E.

## Required Disclosures

### General Information
- Factors used to identify reportable segments
- Types of products and services from which each segment derives revenues

### For Each Reportable Segment

**Profit or Loss Information**:
- Segment profit or loss
- Revenues from external customers
- Intersegment revenues
- Interest revenue
- Interest expense
- Depreciation, depletion, amortization
- Unusual items
- Equity in net income of investees
- Income tax expense or benefit
- Significant noncash items

**Asset Information**:
- Segment assets

### Reconciliations Required

Reconcile segment totals to consolidated amounts:
1. **Total segment revenues** to **consolidated revenues**
2. **Total segment profit/loss** to **consolidated income before tax**
3. **Total segment assets** to **consolidated assets**
4. Other material reconciling items

### Example Reconciliation

```
Segment Revenues:
  Segment A                 $100M
  Segment B                   80M
  Segment C                   50M
  Segment D                   30M
  ─────────────────────────────
  Total segment revenue      260M
  Eliminate intersegment     (25M)
  Other segments (below      15M
    reporting threshold)
  ─────────────────────────────
Consolidated revenue        $255M
                            ═════
```

## Entity-Wide Disclosures

### 1. Revenues by Product/Service
Report revenues from external customers for each product/service (or groups)
- **Even if not organized by product segments**

### 2. Revenues by Geography

Report:
- **(a)** Revenues from domestic operations
- **(b)** Revenues from all foreign countries in total
- **(c)** Revenues from individual foreign countries if material

### 3. Major Customer Disclosure

**If any single customer ≥ 10% of total revenues**:
- Disclose that fact
- Total revenues from that customer
- Identity of segment(s) reporting the revenues

**Note**: Do NOT need to disclose customer's identity

### Example - Major Customer Disclosure

> "Revenues from one customer represent approximately $35 million (14% of total revenues) and are included in Segment A and Segment B."

## CPA Exam Tips

1. **10% tests**:
   - **Revenue test**: Use **TOTAL** revenue (including intersegment)
   - **Profit/loss test**: Use **absolute value** and compare to **greater** of profitable or loss total
   - **Assets test**: Straightforward comparison
   - Meet **ANY ONE** → reportable

2. **75% test**:
   - Use **EXTERNAL revenue only**
   - Must be ≥ 75% of consolidated revenue
   - If not met, add more segments

3. **Common mistakes**:
   - Using external revenue only for revenue test (should include intersegment)
   - Comparing loss to wrong benchmark in profit/loss test
   - Forgetting absolute value in profit/loss test
   - Using internal revenue for 75% test (should be external only)

4. **Most tested**:
   - Applying 10% tests to identify reportable segments
   - Calculating whether 75% threshold is met
   - Understanding what disclosures are required

5. **Reconciliations**: Always required from segment totals to consolidated amounts

## Common Mistakes

1. **Revenue test - using external only**: Should include intersegment revenue

2. **Profit/loss test - wrong comparison**:
   - Must use greater of profitable or loss total
   - Must use absolute value

3. **75% test - using total revenue**: Should use external revenue only

4. **Forgetting to test all three criteria**: Must test revenue, profit/loss, AND assets

5. **Not understanding "any one" rule**: Meeting just one test makes segment reportable

## Summary

### Key Points

- **Management approach**: Segments based on CODM's internal view
- **Reportable if meets ANY ONE 10% test**: Revenue, profit/loss, or assets
- **Revenue test**: Includes **intersegment** revenue
- **Profit/loss test**: Absolute value vs. **greater** of profitable or loss total
- **75% test**: Reportable segments' **external** revenue ≥ 75% of total external revenue
- **Reconciliations required**: Segment totals to consolidated amounts
- **Major customer**: Disclose if ≥ 10% of total revenues

### Decision Framework

**Step 1**: Calculate combined totals
**Step 2**: Calculate 10% thresholds
**Step 3**: Test each segment (any one test passes = reportable)
**Step 4**: Apply 75% test (external revenue only)
**Step 5**: Add segments if needed to meet 75%

### Quick Reference Table

| Test | Formula | Includes Intersegment? |
|------|---------|----------------------|
| **Revenue** | Segment Revenue ≥ 10% × Combined Revenue | **YES** |
| **Profit/Loss** | \|Segment P/L\| ≥ 10% × Greater(Profit, Loss) | N/A |
| **Assets** | Segment Assets ≥ 10% × Combined Assets | N/A |
| **75% Test** | Reportable External Revenue ≥ 75% × Total Revenue | **NO (external only)** |
