# Related Party Transactions and Disclosures

## Definition
Parties that can exercise **control** or **significant influence** over entity

## Examples of Related Parties
- Parent and subsidiaries
- Affiliates
- Principal owners (>10% voting interest)
- Management
- Immediate family of above

## Accounting for Related Party Transactions

### General Measurement
At **exchange amounts** (consideration actually paid/received)

### Exception - Nonmonetary Exchanges
Between related parties may be at **carrying amount** if transaction lacks commercial substance

### No Gain Recognition
**Cannot recognize gain** on related party transaction that lacks commercial substance

## Disclosure Requirements

### Required Disclosures
- Nature of relationship
- Description of transactions
- Dollar amounts
- Amounts due to/from related parties
- Terms and manner of settlement

## Nonmonetary Exchanges Between Related Parties

### Accounting
Record at **carrying amount (book value)** if lacks commercial substance

### No Gain
Do **not** recognize gain

### Example
Parent trades equipment (BV $50K, FV $80K) to subsidiary for land (FV $80K)
- Record land at $50K (carrying amount)
- No gain recognized

## Arm's Length

### Definition
Transaction between unrelated parties in open market

### Related Party Concern
May NOT be at arm's length
- May not reflect fair value
- Increased disclosure scrutiny

## CPA Exam Tips

1. **Related parties**: Disclose relationship and transaction details

2. **Nonmonetary exchanges between related parties**: Carrying amount, no gain

3. **Focus on disclosure requirements** for exam

4. **Cannot recognize gain** on related party transaction without commercial substance

## Common Exam Scenarios

1. Identify related parties in given situation

2. Determine disclosure requirements

3. Account for nonmonetary exchange between parent and subsidiary

## Summary

### Key Points
- Related parties: Control or significant influence relationship
- Disclosure: Relationship, transactions, amounts, terms
- Nonmonetary exchanges lacking commercial substance: Carrying amount, no gain
- Cannot recognize gain on related party transaction without commercial substance
