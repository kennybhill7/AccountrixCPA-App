# Discontinued Operations - ASC 205-20

## Definition
Component of entity that has been disposed of or classified as held for sale and represents a **strategic shift** with **major effect** on operations and financial results.

## Qualifying Criteria

### Must Meet BOTH:
1. **Component of entity**: Operations and cash flows clearly distinguishable from rest of entity
2. **Strategic shift**: Disposal represents strategic shift that has (or will have) **major effect** on entity's operations and financial results

### Examples of Strategic Shift
- Disposal of major geographic area
- Disposal of major line of business
- Disposal of major equity method investment
- Disposal of major business unit or product line

## Classification Triggers

### When to Classify as Discontinued
- Component has been **disposed of** (sold, abandoned, spun off)
- Component classified as **held for sale** (meets held-for-sale criteria per ASC 360)

### Timing
Classify as discontinued in period disposal occurs or held-for-sale criteria met

## Income Statement Presentation

### Separate Section
Present discontinued operations in **separate section AFTER** income from continuing operations

### Two Components
1. **Results of operations**: Income or loss from operations of discontinued component (from beginning of year to disposal/classification date)
2. **Gain/loss on disposal**: Gain or loss on disposal of component (or impairment loss if held for sale)

### Format
```
Income from continuing operations (before tax)      $XXX
Income tax expense                                   (XX)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Income from continuing operations (after tax)        XXX

Discontinued operations:
  Loss from operations of discontinued component    $(XX)
  Loss on disposal of component                      (XX)
  Income tax benefit                                   XX
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Loss from discontinued operations, net of tax        (XX)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Net income                                          $XXX
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### Reported Net of Tax
Discontinued operations reported **NET OF TAX** (calculate tax effect separately)

### EPS Disclosure
Disclose EPS for:
- Continuing operations
- Discontinued operations
- Net income

## Measurement

### If Disposed
Measure at **actual proceeds** received less costs to sell

### If Held for Sale
Measure at **LOWER OF**:
- Carrying amount, OR
- Fair value less cost to sell

Recognize impairment loss if (FV - cost to sell) < carrying amount

### Subsequent Adjustments
Adjustments to prior estimates of gain/loss included in discontinued operations in current period

## Ongoing Involvement

### Significant Continuing Involvement
May **NOT qualify** as discontinued operation if entity has significant continuing involvement in operations after disposal

### Examples of Disqualifying Involvement
- Significant continuing revenue/profit relationship
- Active involvement in operations
- Guarantee of performance

## Calculation Example 1: Disposal

### Facts
- Company disposes of Western Division (meets discontinued ops criteria) on Oct 1, Year 3
- Jan-Sept operating loss: $200,000
- Disposal proceeds: $1,000,000
- Carrying amount: $1,200,000 (loss $200,000)
- Tax rate: 30%

### Operating Loss (After Tax)
```
Operating loss                           $200,000
Tax benefit (30%)                         (60,000)
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Net operating loss                       $140,000
```

### Disposal Loss (After Tax)
```
Disposal loss ($1.2M - $1M)              $200,000
Tax benefit (30%)                         (60,000)
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Net disposal loss                        $140,000
```

### Income Statement Presentation
```
Discontinued operations:
  Loss from operations of Western Division     $(200,000)
  Loss on disposal of Western Division          (200,000)
  Income tax benefit                              120,000
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Loss from discontinued operations, net of tax  $(280,000)
```

## Calculation Example 2: Held for Sale

### Facts
- Eastern Division classified as held for sale on Dec 1, Year 3
- Operating income Jan-Nov: $300,000
- Carrying amount: $2,000,000
- Fair value: $1,800,000
- Cost to sell: $100,000
- Tax rate: 30%

### Impairment Calculation
```
Carrying amount                         $2,000,000
Fair value less cost to sell:
  FV $1,800,000 - Cost $100,000          1,700,000
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Impairment loss                         $  300,000
```

### After-Tax Calculations
```
Operating income: $300,000 × (1 - 0.30) =  $210,000
Impairment loss:  $300,000 × (1 - 0.30) = $(210,000)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Net effect                                      $0
```

### Income Statement Presentation
```
Discontinued operations:
  Income from operations of Eastern Division    $300,000
  Impairment loss on Eastern Division           (300,000)
  Income tax expense (benefit)                         0
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Income from discontinued operations, net of tax       $0
```

## Comparative Statements

### Prior Periods
**Restate** prior period financial statements to separately present discontinued operations

### Continuing Operations
Prior periods restated to show only continuing operations in main income statement

### Note
All periods presented must reclassify discontinued component results to separate section

## Balance Sheet Presentation

### Assets Held for Sale
Present separately on balance sheet (current assets if disposal expected within one year)

### Liabilities Held for Sale
Present separately if disposal group includes liabilities

### No Offsetting
Assets and liabilities of disposal group **not offset** (show separately)

## CPA Exam Tips

1. Discontinued ops requires **BOTH**: (1) Component disposal **AND** (2) Strategic shift with major effect

2. Present discontinued ops **AFTER** income from continuing operations

3. Discontinued ops shown **NET OF TAX**

4. **Two components**: (1) Operating results, (2) Gain/loss on disposal (or impairment)

5. If held for sale: Impairment = Carrying amount - (FV - cost to sell), if positive

6. **Restate prior periods** to reclassify discontinued component

7. Don't confuse with **held-for-sale classification** (held for sale ≠ discontinued unless also strategic shift)

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Strategic Shift Requirement
**Wrong**: Classifying any component disposal as discontinued operations
**Correct**: Must represent **strategic shift** with major effect

### Mistake 2: Tax Effect
**Wrong**: Forgetting to calculate separate tax effects for discontinued ops
**Correct**: Discontinued ops presented **net of tax**

### Mistake 3: Held for Sale vs. Discontinued
**Wrong**: Assuming held-for-sale automatically means discontinued operations
**Correct**: Held for sale is **balance sheet classification**; discontinued is **income statement classification** (requires strategic shift)

### Mistake 4: Prior Periods
**Wrong**: Not restating prior periods
**Correct**: **Restate** all prior periods presented

## Summary

### Qualification Checklist
✓ Component with distinct operations and cash flows?
✓ Disposal represents strategic shift?
✓ Major effect on operations and financial results?

**If all yes** → Discontinued operations

### Key Points
- **Discontinued ops** = Component disposal representing **strategic shift** with major effect
- Present in separate section **AFTER** continuing operations, **NET OF TAX**
- **Two components**: (1) Operating results, (2) Disposal gain/loss or impairment
- If held for sale: Lower of CA or (FV - cost to sell) - recognize impairment if needed
- **Restate prior periods** to reclassify discontinued component
- Separate EPS disclosure for continuing and discontinued operations

### Formula
```
Impairment (if held for sale) = Carrying Amount - (Fair Value - Cost to Sell)
                                 (if result is positive)
```
